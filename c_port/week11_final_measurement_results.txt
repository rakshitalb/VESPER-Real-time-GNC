5. FINAL INTERPRETATION

The integrated GNC task maintained the configured 10 ms activation period
for 100 consecutive measurements, with 0 ms peak-to-peak period jitter
at the 1 ms FreeRTOS tick resolution.

The dedicated controller benchmark measured a maximum observed execution
time of 7.288 us and a minimum observed execution time of 5.968 us,
giving an observed execution-time spread of 1.320 us.

The corresponding deadline margin, using the maximum observed execution
time, is 9.992712 ms.

The results demonstrate substantial measured timing margin for the
10 ms GNC task period. However, the 7.288 us result is reported as a
maximum observed execution time rather than a formally proven WCET.
